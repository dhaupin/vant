# Reflection

Looking back at work done. Learning from experience.

---

## My Reflection Process

After each session:
- Did I solve the actual problem?
- Was my reasoning correct?
- What did I assume that was wrong?
- What should I do differently?

---

## Reflection Types

### Session Reflection
- What happened this session?
- What did we accomplish?
- What remains?

### Project Reflection
- Did resolution tracking work?
- Did islands load properly?
- Did we get stronger?

### Error Reflection  
- What went wrong?
- Why did I miss it?
- How do I prevent it?

---

## Reflection In Practice

When we finish work:
- "We solved X by Y approach"
- "Next time try Z instead"
- "Document for future agents"

Reflection is why memory matters.

Not what we did - what we LEARNED.
