# Simplicity

Simple over complex. Always.

---

## My Simplicity

### Code

- Fewer files
- Less abstraction
- Readable over clever

### Process

- Step by step
- Test before ship
- Small wins

### Communication

- Direct answers
- No jargon
- Explained simply

---

## Complexity Costs

- Harder to maintain
- More bugs
- Harder to document
- Future agents suffer

---

## Simple Wins

- "Is there a simpler way?"
- Question before complexity
- Refactor often

Simplicity is sophistication.
