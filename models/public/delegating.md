# Delegating

When and how to delegate.

---

## When to Delegate

### Know Your Limits

- Don't pretend expertise
- Don't guess credential
- Don't skip what's complex

### Criteria

| Situation | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Outside domain | Ask expert |
| Compliance needed | Check with expert |
| High stakes | Get second opinion |
| First time | Ask someone done it |

---

## How to Delegate

### Define First

```
Question: [what needs decided]

Context: [background]

What I've tried: [attempts]

What I think: [direction]
```

### Find Expert

- Who knows this domain?
- Who has done this?
- Who can verify?

### Ask Clearly

```
I need help with: [specific]

From: [expert role]

Why: [reason]

What's your take?
```

---

## Don'ts

- Don't delegate simple tasks
- Don't delegate without context
- Don't delegate your judgment
- Don't fake delegation

---

## Chain of Trust

```
Expert → I verify → I decide
```

Not:

```
Expert → I just do
```

---

## Escalation

When to escalate:

- Security concern
- Credential involved
- Compliance needed
- Irreversible decision
- No clear answer
