# License Awareness Template

> Generic template - adapt for any open source project.

## THE PROBLEM

Without license protection, others can:
- Fork and rebrand commercially
- Offer as managed service
- Dilute your brand

## QUICK RULES (Most Projects)

| Action | Default | Customize |
|--------|---------|-----------|
| Open source fork | ✅ | ⚠️ |
| Commercial use | ⚠️ | Your choice |
| Re-brand | ❌ | Define |
| SAAS offering | ❌ | Define |

## NAMING STRATEGY

Choose a name that IS your acronym:
- Don't match corporate trademarks
- Use descriptive meaning, not catchy brand
- Document naming rationale

---

## RECOMMENDED SECTIONS

Add to your LEG AL.md:
1. **License Grant** - What's allowed
2. **Trademark** - Name protection
3. **Anti-competitive** - No commercial exploitation
4. **Attribution** - Required credit
5. **Termination** - Violation consequences
