The Versatile Autonomous Networked Tool (Vant) is a general-purpose, non-commercial open-source developer framework, file-based cold storage architecture, and Model Context Protocol (MCP) server. It functions strictly as an alternative runtime execution environment for software developers building localized JavaScript/TypeScript artificial intelligence entities. Vant is distributed globally under the terms of the MIT open-source license.
